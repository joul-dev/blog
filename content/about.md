I'm Joul, a founder based in Geneva. I run three connected products:

- **[gen-image.com](https://gen-image.com)** — a B2C AI image and video generation platform with a large user base
- **[imference.com](https://imference.com)** — a multi-model inference API
- **[Runqy](https://runqy.com)** — the open-source Go task queue that dispatches GPU workloads behind both

I'm more operator than engineer: I handle product, infrastructure, billing, support and growth, and I lean heavily on AI-assisted development to ship at a pace a small team normally couldn't.

This blog is where I share what that actually looks like — real numbers, real costs, real mistakes.
